I study AI safety and alignment starting from the people these systems are meant to serve. I am most interested in quantitative, qualitative, and philosophical methods to explore questions about the societal impacts, governance, and future of AI and humanity.

I have worked with Dr. Liwei Jiang at the University of Washington on HearSayBench, a benchmark asking whether language models recognize the unstated constraints that make standard advice impossible for people in adversarial conditions, and with Prof. Daryl Cameron at Penn State on empathy and AI risk. My publications can be found here.
